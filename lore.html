G-Toilet
Historical Record

Before the Skibidi Toilet Empire existed, G-Toilet was part of the Astro Empire. After betraying the empire, G-Toilet was expelled to Earth alongside other traitors.

Stripped of his former power and resources, G-Toilet was forced to start over from nothing — with zero upgrades and no empire.

From Earth, he began rebuilding his power and creating a new empire of his own: the Skibidi Toilet Empire.
