Report

Shiro was sent to the Cam Base with the mission of investigating and supervising the progress made by the Cam scientists regarding Titan Speakerman.

Upon arriving, Shiro found the Titan undergoing various upgrades and modifications. However, during the inspection, Titan Speakerman reacted unexpectedly and grabbed Shiro, beginning to release a massive amount of energy that eventually caused him to overload.

The situation quickly spiraled out of control. Shortly after, Uran arrived at the base and intervened, managing to free Shiro from the Titan's grip and stop the overload.

The incident raised questions about Titan Speakerman's new upgrades and whether the Cam scientists truly have complete control over his newly acquired capabilities.
